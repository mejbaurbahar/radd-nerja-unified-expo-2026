1

"RaddTag" — Zero-Code E-Commerce Behavioral Tracker & Settings Ingestion

New Data Foundation

The single highest-leverage missing component in Radd today. Radd is currently "blind" to store visitors until they click the chat widget or send a WhatsApp message. RaddTag solves this entirely.

🔍 WHAT IT IS

A lightweight, 4KB zero-dependency client JavaScript snippet (raddtag.js) embedded into the merchant's store header (Shopify, WooCommerce, Salla, Zid, Custom Web). It auto-detects e-commerce DOM interactions: view_item, add_to_cart, remove_from_cart, begin_checkout, phone_captured, and scroll_milestone without requiring custom merchant coding.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

1. Settings UI: Added under /settings/raddtag with a 1-click copy script snippet and real-time Ping Verifier.
2. DOM Heuristics: Listens to clicks on button[name="add"], form inputs with telephone regex (input[type="tel"]), and Shopify/WooCommerce cart mutations.
3. High-Throughput Ingestion: Batches events and beacons them via navigator.sendBeacon to Radd's /api/v1/events/ingest cluster, mapping anonymous session UUIDs to visitor phone numbers the moment they type in checkout.

💰 WHY IT'S CRITICAL

Without behavioral data, automated recovery is impossible. Over 70% of online shoppers abandon carts, and 92% browse without buying. Capturing these intent signals in real time feeds Radd's automation triggers and gives human agents full shopper context in the inbox.

2

"Auto Funnels" — Autonomous Event-Driven WhatsApp Recovery (Cart & Browse Drops)

Core Revenue Driver

Expands Radd's Broadcast engine from manual scheduled list blasting into continuous event-driven automation recipes.

🔍 WHAT IT IS

A dedicated "Auto Funnels" section in Radd (/funnels and /broadcast/create?mode=automated) offering pre-built autonomous recovery recipes: Cart Abandonment (items left in cart), Browse Hesitation Drop-off (product viewed 3+ times without cart add), and Checkout Stall Alert (phone entered but payment uncompleted).

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

1. Timing Engine: When RaddTag records an abandoned cart event, a delayed job (e.g. 30 mins) is queued in Redis/BullMQ.
2. Exit Condition Check: If an order_completed event arrives before the timer fires, the job auto-cancels.
3. Dynamic Meta Variables: If not completed, Radd automatically triggers a pre-approved Meta WhatsApp utility/marketing template, injecting customer name, item name, image thumbnail, and a tracked 1-click discount link.

💰 WHY IT'S CRITICAL

WhatsApp yields an unmatched 98% open rate and 45–60% CTR compared to email's 20% open rate. Instead of merchants manually crafting one-off broadcasts, Auto Funnels run 24/7 on autopilot, converting lost traffic into paying customers while merchants sleep.

3

"Event Analytics" — Live Event Data Stream & Behavioral Funnel Inspection

Data Intelligence

Provides the dedicated interface where merchants can see every live event captured from their storefront, inspect event volume distribution, and audit store behavioral drop-offs.

🔍 WHAT IT IS

A new Event Analytics navigation page (/event-analytics) mirroring Nerja's Insight AI. Displays total tracked events (e.g. 3,797 events), 30-day behavioral event breakdowns (views vs cart adds vs checkout starts), top viewed vs top purchased products, and a real-time live event telemetry feed.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

1. Real-Time Telemetry: Ingested RaddTag payloads publish to a Redis Pub/Sub channel and persist in PostgreSQL with time-bucket aggregations.
2. WebSocket Live Feed: The page connects to a secure WebSocket channel, updating a live event ticker showing events as they occur on the storefront (e.g. "Visitor in Riyadh added Pro ANC Headset to cart").
3. Behavioral Conversion Funnel: Automatically calculates drop-off rates across: Product Views (100%) ➔ Cart Additions (24.2%) ➔ Checkout Begun (8.6%) ➔ Orders Placed (3.8%).

💰 WHY IT'S CRITICAL

Store owners and marketers demand visibility into what their shoppers are doing. This gives merchants complete trust that RaddTag is active, working, and capturing customer hesitation points with 100% fidelity.

4

"Revenue AI" — Financial Recovery Attribution & Executive ROI Ledger

Executive Monetization

The ultimate executive selling point. Replaces support cost-center metrics with verifiable dollar revenue attributed directly to Radd.

🔍 WHAT IT IS

A dedicated Revenue AI dashboard (/revenue and embedded above /dashboard). Reports exact dollars recovered ($7,699.00), attributed order counts (18 orders), average order value ($427.70), and a transaction ledger detailing every recovered purchase.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

1. Tokenized Checkout Links: Recovery links sent via WhatsApp/Live Chat contain signed tracking parameters (?radd_ref=tok_98a72).
2. Webhook Listener: Radd listens to store webhooks (Shopify orders/create, WooCommerce, Salla).
3. Attribution Windows: Orders placed within 24h to 30 days matching either the phone number or the tracking cookie are credited to Radd and logged into the revenue ledger with order ID, items, and total amount.

💰 WHY IT'S CRITICAL

Software that proves it generates 15.7x ROI ($7,699 revenue vs $199 subscription) never gets cancelled. It empowers Radd to launch higher subscription tiers ($199–$499/mo) with zero friction.

5

"Live Store Copilot" — Real-Time Shopper Cart & Intent Signals in Agent Inbox

Human + AI Superpower

Unifies Radd's core competitive advantage (human agent inbox) with Nerja's event intelligence into a single synchronized workspace.

🔍 WHAT IT IS

A contextual real-time drawer integrated on the right side of Radd's WhatsApp and Live Chat windows (/whatsapp-chat). Displays the visitor's live cart items ($185.00), exact products viewed, checkout drop-off step, customer lifetime spend, and an AI recommendation widget with 1-click snippet injection.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

When an agent opens a conversation, Radd queries the visitor's session history matched by phone number or cookie ID. The copilot displays the real-time event timeline (e.g. "Viewed Headphones at 10:35 AM", "Cart added at 10:36 AM") and suggests optimal replies with personalized discount codes.

💰 WHY IT'S CRITICAL

Eliminates the blind "How can I help you?" greeting. Agents immediately know what the customer wanted, why they stopped checkout, and can close high-value sales in under 60 seconds.

6

"Omnichannel Connectors" — Intelligent WhatsApp ➔ Email ➔ SMS Waterfall Routing

Multi-Channel Infrastructure

Bridges the gap when WhatsApp is unavailable or template limits are reached, ensuring 100% message delivery.

🔍 WHAT IT IS

An integration hub under /settings/integrations allowing merchants to connect verified third-party messaging providers for Email (Resend, AWS SES) and SMS (Twilio, BulkSMS) alongside Radd's native WhatsApp Cloud API.

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

Configurable waterfall rules: When an abandoned cart is detected, Radd attempts delivery via WhatsApp first (highest engagement). If delivery fails or the message remains unread after 45 minutes, the system automatically falls back to an HTML recovery email or SMS text.

💰 WHY IT'S CRITICAL

Guarantees zero dropped revenue opportunities, protects merchants against regional WhatsApp delivery hiccups, and provides true multi-channel enterprise coverage.
