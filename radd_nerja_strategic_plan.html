4. Deep Dive: Top 5 Features to Integrate (What, How & Why)

Comprehensive engineering specifications and business justifications for each capability.

Tier 1 · Critical Priority Data & Behavioral Tracking

Feature 1: "RaddTag" — Zero-Code E-Commerce Behavioral Tracker

🚀 Expected Conversion Uplift: +28%
📌 What is it?
A lightweight JavaScript snippet (modelled after Nerja's t.js) that e-commerce merchants paste into their store's <head> or install via Shopify/WooCommerce/Salla/Zid plugins. It auto-detects:
  • page_view & scroll_milestone
  • view_item (Product title, SKU, Price)
  • add_to_cart & cart_abandoned
  • begin_checkout & phone_captured
⚙️ How to Implement in Radd?
  • In Settings > General: Add a new tab called "Radd Data Brain" or "Store Tracker".
  • Generate an API Key with a public prefix (radd_pk_live_xxxx) and an origin domain allowlist.
  • Provide a 1-line copyable script tag with a live status verifier (e.g. "Receiving events · 4,210 total").
  • Instant Rollout: Merge RaddTag directly into Radd's existing Live Chat script so merchants get tracking automatically with zero setup!
💡 Why do we need it?
Currently, Radd is completely blind to what shoppers are doing on the merchant's website until they manually send a message. By tracking shopper behavior in real-time, Radd can proactively trigger personalized WhatsApp assistance at the exact moment of hesitation.
Tier 1 · Critical Priority Automation & Revenue

Feature 2: Event-Driven WhatsApp Recovery Funnels (Browse & Cart Abandonment)

💰 Immediate Revenue Engine
📌 What is it?
Upgrading Radd's current Broadcast section (/broadcast) from purely manual blast broadcasts to automated, event-triggered sequences:
  • Browse Abandonment: Shopper viewed a product 3+ times without buying → Send personalized WhatsApp message after 30 mins.
  • Cart Abandonment: Added items to cart, but no checkout within 1 hour → Send WhatsApp reminder with dynamic product picture & discount code.
  • Checkout Stalled: Phone number captured at checkout, but purchase not completed → Urgent WhatsApp assistance within 15 mins.
⚙️ How to Implement in Radd?
  • In Radd's Broadcast page, add a toggle: "Scheduled Blast" vs "Autonomous Trigger".
  • Add pre-configured recipe presets just like Nerja: Browse Abandonment, Cart Recovery, Post-Purchase Upsell.
  • Connect dynamic placeholders ({{1}}, {{2}} in Meta approved templates) to cart items (e.g., product title, direct cart checkout URL).
  • When the customer replies on WhatsApp, the conversation lands directly in Radd's live chat inbox with full cart context!
💡 Why do we need it?
Cart abandonment is the #1 pain point of every e-commerce merchant. Traditional emails have an 18-22% open rate, whereas WhatsApp has a 98% open rate and 45-60% click-through rate. This feature alone allows our sales team to pitch Radd as an instant ROI machine.
Tier 2 · High Priority Analytics & Business Intelligence

Feature 3: Financial Revenue & Recovery Attribution Dashboard

📊 Essential for Enterprise Retention
📌 What is it?
A dedicated "Revenue AI" tab (modeled after Nerja's /revenue) that tracks and proves the monetary value generated by Radd:
  • Total Attributed Revenue (e.g. $18,450 recovered)
  • Attributed Orders & Average Order Value (AOV)
  • Campaign ROI Breakdown (WhatsApp campaigns vs Live Chat agent closes)
  • Customer Revenue Ledger (Lifetime value of customers assisted via WhatsApp)
⚙️ How to Implement in Radd?
  • Inject UTM parameters and unique recovery tokens into links sent via WhatsApp & Live Chat.
  • Listen for WooCommerce/Shopify/Custom order.created webhooks and match the customer's phone or email within a configurable attribution window (e.g., 24h, 7d, 30d).
  • Display this on Radd's main dashboard above conversation metrics, so the first thing the merchant sees every morning is: "Radd made you $2,400 this week".
💡 Why do we need it?
Software that proves direct revenue attribution has virtually zero churn. When SaaS budgets get reviewed, support tools get cut, but revenue-generating tools get increased investment. This transforms Radd's market positioning.
Tier 2 · High Priority Agent Intelligence

Feature 4: Live Store Copilot & Real-Time Customer Intent Signals

🤖 AI Superpower for Support Agents
📌 What is it?
Nerja features a live "Agent Walking Your Store" feed and an "Intent Signals" studio showing visitors browsing, items hovered, and high-intent drop-offs.

In Radd, when an agent opens a chat with a customer, they will see a real-time behavioral side panel:
  • Currently viewing: Wireless Mechanical Keyboard ($89.99)
  • Time spent on checkout: 4 mins 12 sec
  • Cart Value: $179.98 (2 items)
⚙️ How to Implement in Radd?
  • In Radd's WhatsApp Chat & Live Chat split view, expand the right-hand metadata drawer.
  • Add a "Live Browsing Journey" widget that fetches real-time session events from RaddTag matching that user's phone or email.
  • Provide AI Agent 1-click recommendations: "Customer hesitated on shipping price — offer free shipping coupon?"
💡 Why do we need it?
Support agents currently respond blindly without knowing why the customer reached out. With real-time context, agents can close sales in seconds with precise answers, radically shortening resolution times and delighting customers.
Tier 3 · Strategic Advantage Channel Redundancy

Feature 5: Omnichannel Fallback Routing (Email + SMS Connectors)

🛡️ 100% Reachability Guarantee
📌 What is it?
Nerja includes pre-built connectors for Resend, Amazon SES, Twilio SMS, and BulkSMSBD alongside WhatsApp.

Radd should offer smart omnichannel fallback:
  • Primary Channel: WhatsApp (98% Open Rate)
  • If WhatsApp template fails (unregistered or blocked) → Fallback to SMS.
  • If phone number is absent but email was captured at checkout → Send branded Email.
⚙️ How to Implement in Radd?
  • In Settings > General, create a new Connectors sub-page.
  • Allow merchants to plug in their Resend / SendGrid / Twilio API keys.
  • In Broadcast / Automation setup, allow a multi-step waterfall: "Send WhatsApp. If not delivered within 30 mins, send Email."
💡 Why do we need it?
Meta WhatsApp charges per conversation and occasionally rejects marketing templates or encounters delivery issues. Having email/SMS fallback ensures 0% message loss and provides enterprise clients with complete peace of mind.
