## 🎯 Executive Vision (Ammar Ahmed's Directives)

*"Giving solution in one place... The communication channels become selling channels. Also, we don't only do customer support... we have customer support with AI, plus selling products and services, plus a marketing tool to analyze where customers stop and re-engage them."*  
— **Ammar Ahmed**, Strategic Planning Session

### The Three Core Pillars
1. **AI Customer Support (Radd AI)**: 24/7 intelligent automated resolution across Meta WhatsApp WABA, Web Live Chat, Instagram, and Twitter/X with human agent escalation.
2. **The Selling Tool (In-Chat Commerce)**: Direct sales in messaging channels—purchase products, book hotel rooms, schedule clinic consultations, or order restaurant food with native Mada / Apple Pay links.
3. **Nerja Marketing Engine (Nerja AI)**: Predictive intent tracking identifying drop-off points, behavioral scoring, and automated re-engagement triggers.

- **Radd AI Gateway**: Multi-tenant workspace engine (713 registered companies), Meta Cloud API Tier-4 connection, AWS Lambda & Redis queue dispatches.
